# Puck

Puck is a small, keyboard-friendly companion in the Omarchy top bar. Click
the animated eyes in the bar to open it.

- Keep a short todo list and mark tasks complete without leaving the bar.
- Save useful links; clicking one opens it in your browser.
- Check in water with a bottle button (including a visible daily count), a
  small walk (which toggles the fullscreen Omarchy screensaver), or an
  ear/volume break.
- Receive gentle, rate-limited reminders for water, movement, headphone
  volume, unfinished todos, Night Light in the evening, and high Codex usage.
- If the default output stays at 95%+ for 30 minutes, Puck lowers it to 70%
  and leaves an appropriately cheeky notification.

All data stays on this computer in:

```
~/.local/state/puck/data.json
```

The widget's settings in Omarchy let you tune reminder intervals and the
Night Light prompt hour. In the panel, press `A` to add a todo, `W` for water,
`B` for a walk and screensaver, `E` for an ears break, `N` to toggle Night
Light, `R` to refresh, or `Esc` to close.
